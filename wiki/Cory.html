Cory the engineer is found in the far northwestern part of The Village and north of the Beekeeper. During the day, he is outside and sells stones and stone products to you. At night he goes inside and is unavailable without 80. He's the brother of Tress, the carpenter.

Stranger Sins

Quests

Cory and Tress need to help you build your Tavern after buying the land from Horadric for 30 . Speaking to Cory he will tell you they need to build a barn first. You can choose to either pay him (2 ) or supply him the materials (12
Simple iron parts, 12
Wooden plank, 12
Nails and 50 ) to finish working on the barn and get to building your Tavern. Go to sleep to let the brothers finish the Tavern for you. You'll receive the Talking Skull achievement.
