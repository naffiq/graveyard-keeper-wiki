Better Save Soul Description

We're all sinners - as the Bishop in the Town teaches us. Our new acquaintance Euric though is set on proving the opposite. And he will of course need the help of the Graveyard Keeper! Prepare your tools, your charm, and put your mind to work on a mission to save souls!

No one is without sin - this is what the Bishop in the Town teaches, and it is true. But who said that one cannot be cleansed from these sins? Help your new friend Euric to fulfill his cherished dream and remove the shards of sins from his soul. In return, he is ready to share a book, which contains knowledge for remote craft control of workbenches... What are you ready to do to get it?

Note: The „Better Save Soul“ DLC is not available on Nintendo Switch.

Questions

Do I need to replay the main game?

As with previous DLCs it is not required to start a new savefile. To Start the DLC open the Church and repair the Corpse Hatch. Starting the Bowl quest from the Bishop is also required.

Are there new achievements?

For a list of achievements added by the DLC please see here

What should we expect in the DLC?

  • 6-12 hours of extra gameplay
  • Manage your workbenches directly from the map with the remote craft control
  • Save souls for the sake of The Ancient Contract (and your own profit)
  • Help to fulfill the dream of your new shady friend - Euric
  • Discover a new area for all the new workbenches

Free or Paid DLC?

Paid.

Release dates

Better Save Soul was officially announced on 22.10.2021. Better Save Soul released on 27.10.2021.

The existence of a new DLC named "Better Save Soul" was revealed via Steam records on October 11th 2021.[1] The DLCs Store page was created on 20.10.2021.
