Basic gameplay

Controls

Esc - Pause

W - Up

S - Down

A - Left

D - Right

E - Interact

F - Work

Space - Attack with sword

Tab - Game menu

I - Inventory

T - Technologies

R - rotate

N - Known NPCs

M - Map
