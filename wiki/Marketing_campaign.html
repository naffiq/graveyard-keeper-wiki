Marketing campaign
Basic information
Related pages :
 Merchant
Location(s)
Trade Office

A marketing campaign is started at the desk of the trade office and advertises your goods in the town. You need to make 10×
flyers, to earn yourself 1×
fame. With that fame, you are able to build another crate pallet.

You can also get 1 fame when you complete the quest where you give Ms. Charm a piece of Jewelry (Merchants questline)

Before being able to enter the trade office, you've got to advance the merchant's quest.
