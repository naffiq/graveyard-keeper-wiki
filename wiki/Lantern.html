Graveyard, Forest near the river, Cabin in the woods

The lantern can either be constructed on the graveyard, or on a place for lantern in the forest near the river or near the cabin in the woods. You need different technologies for this.

Lanterns automatically ignite at dusk and go off at dawn. They shed some light at the surroundings.

Note: Those belonging to the lantern network located in the forest can not be removed. There are 2 alternative buildings: a Torch and a Wooden lantern.

Lanterns give static +4 when built on the graveyard. The maximum of 9 lanterns share the same 9 slots with the
Flowerbed,
Wooden prayer station and
Stone prayer station in the graveyard.
