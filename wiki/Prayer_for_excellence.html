Prayer for Excellence grants the Handyman mood effect, increasing the quality of a few types of crafting by 20% (+0.2 quality). It does not buff foods, drinks, or writing. Stacks with several perks, such as Engineer. Otherwise, the sermon provides a nominal boost to faith and donations, depending on the quality. It is generally not worth using unless you have a specific thing you want to make at a higher quality for fewer material resources, such as a chisel.
