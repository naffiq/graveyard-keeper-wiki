Carrot seeds are one of five different types of basic seeds, to be utilized at the Kitchen Garden. They must be planted in an empty garden bed and may be harvested after a certain amount of time. In order to obtain carrot seeds, you will likely need to purchase them from the Farmer, though there is a small chance in finding some within the Dungeon. However, you will receive a small amount after gaining access to the Church - a "gift" from the Donkey.

Harvesting a mature crop will return some seeds, though without using a quality fertilizer, the chance of receiving enough seeds to replant the crop is very unlikely. See the relevant articles on each fertilizer - or the general article on farming - for more info.
