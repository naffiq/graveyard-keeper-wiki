Burned House

The Burned House, located on a hill north-east of Sweet Home, was destroyed in the Great blast, formerly the residence of the Inquisitor. The ruin is relatively unremarkable, aside from a grave in the back yard, presumably the resting place of the Inquisitor's wife. It is also mentioned by the Inquisitor that he had lost a child (Snake) in the fire, but was never able to find the body.

The grave itself doesn't appear to have much significance. Upon discovery, there is a damaged wooden marker and wooden grave fence pre-installed, which the player can repair, though with no known consequence.
