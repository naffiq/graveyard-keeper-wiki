The Goldfish is the rarest and most valuable fish in the game. It can either be sold to the Lighthouse Keeper or prepared at the cooking table. But due to the high sell price, it is better to sell for profit.

Fishing

The odds of catching any fish are primarily affected by the other fish in the same area, and what each are interested in; more competition between fish, less of a chance in catching a particular fish. In addition, here is a table of this fish's preferences by the rod used, the chosen bait, the location you choose to fish, the area you choose to cast, and sometimes even the time of day.

The Goldfish is incredibly difficult to catch. Even with the best equipment, the player's odds at successfully obtaining one are only 2% during the day and 5% at night.
