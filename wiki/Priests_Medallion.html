Priests Medallion

Priests Medallion
General Information
Type :
Stranger Sins Quest item
Source(s)
Dig


The Priests Medallion is received from Dig. Upon speaking with Dig, he'll ask you to get him some booze. You can either make some or speak to Snake to get some. Give the booze to Dig and he'll check it for quality by lighting it on fire and then give you the Priests Medallion.
