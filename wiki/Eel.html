The Eel is one of sixteen(16) different 'fish' that can be caught, and either sold to the Lighthouse Keeper, processed for cooking, or - in it's particular case, once studied - processed into a powder for use in alchemy. The difficulty in catching it is relatively low, thus it's thought to be a relatively low-tier fish.
