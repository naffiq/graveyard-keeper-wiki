Wheat is a form of crop which can be grown from Wheat seeds in the garden below the workyard known as the kitchen garden. Alternatively it can be purchased from the Miller. Wheat is not an improvable crop, meaning it does not have a variety of quality levels and cannot be improved with fertilizer. It can only be made to grow faster using boost fertilizers. See the page on farming for more information on how crops are grown and improved.
