Sermon

The ability to perform sermons is unlocked when you get the Preacher perk as part of the story progression. The Preacher perk is obtained when you speak to the bishop on after having achieved a graveyard quality of 5 or higher.

To perform a sermon, wait for until sunrise then go to the church, stand behind the lectern and press the action button (E) to pray, then select the sermon/prayer to perform, a short cutscene will play out showing a reaction of the congregation and giving you an idea of how the sermon went, afterward the members of the congregation will all provide a donation based on your Graveyard Rating and Church Rating .

Note: If you already have performed a service that day, you'll get an error message, saying that you have to wait until next . This is misleading before sunrise, because then, it says next too, but it's actually only waiting for sunrise.

Church Influence

For the main article on how church ratings work see church.

Each sermon you can perform has a requirement to be 100% successful. You can still perform a sermon in a church below that requirement but it will have a chance to fail. Your church rating determines how many parishioners will attend your sermon as well as the amount of faith you will get as a result of your sermon. A successful sermon creates donations at the base rate of 3:1, and will produce faith at a rate of 1:5.

Sermon Topics

All sermon topics must first be unlocked via various technologies and then must be crafted at the desk for use in a sermon. The exception to this is the casual prayer which is given by default when the church is first opened.

A successful sermon provides additional faith and coins specified on the prayer. Otherwise, you'll just get the base results.

Crafting Sermons

Where to craft :

  • Sermons are crafted at Desk and Desk II.
  • Notes and Chapters required are also crafted at the desks.
  • Books required by some of them are crafted at the desks.
  • Hard cover and Softcover required to make the Books are crafted at the Church workbench.
  • Clean paper is produced in different ways at different stations.

Desk II is not required for anything but it improves the quality of many crafts compared to Desk.

Quality of notes, chapters and most sermons is impacted by:

  • The base difficulty of the craft
  • Writer perk : +30%
  • Playwright perk : +50%
  • Inspiration buff from Prayer for imagination : +70%
  • Desk II : 0 to 30%, 20% for most sermons ( See Desk II for details )
  • Quality of the Ingredients : using only the best ingredients gives +200 compared to using only the worst. Each quality uptick of an ingredient is worth the same. (So for a craft with a single ingredient with quality and that quality ranging from Bronze to Gold, each uptick is worth 100%.)

Quality of Prayer for imagination and Prayer for excellence is not improved by anything but the ingredients.

Quality of Book is improved by Desk II (20%) and Jeweler perk (70%)
