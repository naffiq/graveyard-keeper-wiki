Grape seeds are one of five different types of complex seeds, to be utilized at the Vineyard. They must be planted in a vine trellis and may be harvested after a certain amount of time. In order to obtain grape seeds, you will likely need to purchase them from the Merchant, though there is a small chance in finding some within the Dungeon.

Harvesting a mature crop will return some seeds, though without using a quality fertilizer, the chance of receiving enough seeds to replant the crop is very unlikely. In addition, given the varieties of quality, the use of Q1 and Q2 fertilizers will provide small amounts of upgraded produce and seeds. Furthermore, given the lengthy maturation time of this particular crop, a dedicated Boost fertilizer is often encouraged. See the relevant articles on each fertilizer - or the general article on farming - for more info.
