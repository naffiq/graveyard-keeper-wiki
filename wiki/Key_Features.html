Key Features

Key Features

Graveyard Keeper is a game with many features that the eponymous Keeper can partake in, and the most prominent of those are listed here.

  • Technologies. To progress through the game, you must go through the technology tree, unlocking Perks and recipes to increase efficiency and profit.
  • Farming. Farming is a pillar that allows you to make food to restore energy, and grow crops to make food for quests and profit.
  • The Graveyard. The graveyard itself is, naturally, a big part of the game. Increasing your graveyard rating will allow you to make more money from Sermons, which is a main source of revenue.
  • Alchemy. Alchemy allows you to make potions to temporarily increase attributes, and make ingredients for quests.
  • The Church. As a Graveyard Keeper, you are also a preacher, using Sermons to gain benefits and money, as well as upgrading the church to get more Faith.
  • Corpses are naturally a big part of the game. You can Embalm them and 12-Skull Corpse Preparation prepare them, to ensure that only the best bodies get in your very exclusive graveyard,

There are also many more minor features that nevertheless feature in the game, such as Fishing, reanimating Zombies, Cremation, and the many quests provided by NPCs and other characters.

DLC Key Features

In Stranger sins, a major feature added is Tavern management , allowing you to host events and sell alcohol at your very own tavern In the Game of Crone DLC, you aid a struggling Refugee camp, and build it into a fortified settlement, unlocking new recipes, perks, and vendors.
