Cabbage is a crop grown from cabbage seeds in the garden below the workyard in the kitchen garden. It can be purchased from the Farmer. Consuming cabbage provides 5. 12 Cabbages are required for the Merchant's quest. They do not have to be grown by the player. Cabbage is not an improvable crop. It does not have quality levels and cannot be improved with fertilizer. It can only be made to grow faster. See farming for more information on how crops are grown and improved.
