Simple iron parts are an intermediate crafting resource made from iron ingots at the anvil, and stack to a total of 50. Alternatively they can be purchased from the Blacksmith or found by breaking barrels and furniture in the dungeon and cellar. As the Wooden anvil used to craft the simple iron parts requires simple iron parts, you will most likely need to either purchase some from the Blacksmith initially or get lucky and find them in the cellar. You receive 5×
Simple iron parts as a reward from Krezvold's quest, Kill Slimes.
