Silver nuggets are a relatively rare resource that can be obtained through a variety of methods. With the Jeweler technology, they can be processed into Silver ingots at a Furnace II or Furnace III, which currently may only be sold to the Merchant for money. Once a single nugget has been studied, nuggets can be broken down into a Silver powder for use in alchemy.

Sources

  • By mining and processing iron ore after opening the Precious Metals technology:
    • mining iron ore blocks from the Iron Deposit, located in the north-west corner of the map, near the Quarry. Chance is 20% per block with the Miner perk (tested on PC version 1.407).
    • processing these blocks into ore at a stone cutter. A 30% chance to get 2 nuggets from each block, the same chance for both Stone cutter and Stone cutter II (tested on PC version 1.407).
  • While exploring the dungeon, you may come across:
    • silver ore veins, requiring a pickaxe to mine, each yielding 8 nuggets, or 9 if the Jeweler perk is unlocked.
    • Silver Golems on levels 13 and 14, dropping 2-5 nuggets each.
  • By processing the silver crucian fish at the cooking table, each yielding 1 nugget.
  • From the Moneylender in the Refugee camp for 7 (with the Game of Crone DLC).

It is not possible to find any precious metals in the bog iron deposits.
