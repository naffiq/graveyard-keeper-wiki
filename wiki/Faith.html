Faith

Faith
General Information
Type :
Church Item
Source(s)
Sermons, Confessional

Faith is a resource that can be obtained either through holding sermons on or occasionally at random from a confessional. Later in the game, you can also obtain some faith at the burnings.

Faith is required to study most objects in the study table as well as for some NPC interactions. It is used in crafting sermons, advanced stone works and Zombies.

Quests

  • Snake requires 5×
    Faith     before he will interact with the player.
  • Ms. Charm wants 5×
    Faith     before really talking to the player.
