Grapes are a type of crop which can be grown from grape seeds in the vineyard. Grapes are an improvable crop, meaning they have a variety of quality levels and can be improved upon with fertilizer. The quality of the grapes will affect the quality of the products made from it. For more information, see farming.

Trading

Despite the Merchant being involved in trading the seeds, he is not interested in the produce, nor is any other known vendor. Regarding goods derived from grapes that can be bought or sold, see grape pie and red wine.
