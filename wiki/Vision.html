Vision

The visions are granted by an anchient machine that is located in the Talking Skull Cellar. Once Garry askes you to find some booze, you have to dig northern of Dig to find the anchient machine. Sadly you're not allowed to take it with you, because it's not your land, so you have to buy this plot from Horadric in Digs name. Now you can build the Talking Skull in which the machine is located in the cellar.

To get the machine to work, you have to get some artifacts. You have to get them one by one and insert them into the machine to get a corresponding vision. Gerry tells you, where to find the next artifacts after you've seen a vision.
