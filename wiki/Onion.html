Onions are a form of crop which can be grown from onion seeds in the garden below the workyard known as the kitchen garden. Alternatively they can be purchased from the Farmer. Onions are an improvable crop, meaning they have a variety of quality levels and can be improved with fertilizer. See the page on farming for more information on how crops are grown and improved.

Alchemy

Onions can be decomposed to powder or extract at the Hand mixer and any of the distillation cubes, respectively. Having unlocked Master of Alchemy produces one additional reagent at each workstation.
