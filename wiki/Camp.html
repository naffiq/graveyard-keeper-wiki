The gypsy camp is located in the northeast of the village. The road from the chicken farm to the mountain fort leads there. It consists of four tents and a wagon around a campfire. There's also a barrel on wheels and a handcart.

This is, where the gypsies entered the region and have put up camp. They are unable to leave, because the mountain path is blocked. The gypsy baron is still there, his sister Esmeralda and his niece Aleidita are no longer at the camp. One of the gypies was also arrested by the Inquisitor.

You can't interact with the buildings there, but the gypsy baron has interesting stories to tell. A few quests take place here and the baron has a cooking recipe for you.
