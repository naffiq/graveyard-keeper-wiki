Location(s)
Cabin in the woods

The lantern network can be constructed using the workbench near the cabin in the woods. It sets up eleven places where you can build lanterns. They follow the path down from the cabin in the woods, across the footbridge, through the forest, past the felling site, around the apiary and on to the workyard.

Cost

Setting up the network is free, but building the lanterns needs some materials.

Benefit

Provides light so you can see.

Does not affect bat spawns.
