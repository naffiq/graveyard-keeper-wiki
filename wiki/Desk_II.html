The desk II is a workstation with the function of crafting various quality paper products and sermons. It's an upgraded Desk featuring identical recipes, but with discounted energy costs and/or increased quality.

The increase of quality in the production of most prayers compared to Desk is 20%. In other words, if a craft on Desk had 30% chance to get you a Silver quality result and 70% chance for Bronze, the same ingredients on Desk II will get you a 50% chance for silver.
