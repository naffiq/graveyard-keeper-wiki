Location information

The crematorium is an outdoor location which can be found south of the entrance to the morgue, approximately one screen away. The location offers room for:

1×
trunk
4×
place for burning corpses

Cremation of any corpse is performed by placing the body on a prepared place for burning corpses and setting it on fire. The process - taking a mere 0:20 seconds - destroys the corpse, uses up the pyre, and leaves behind:

1×
burial certificate
5×
ash
2×
salt

The amount of retrievable materials is not dependent on the remaining body parts, meaning a fully extracted body with no body parts left inside will yield the same amount of ash as a corpse with all body parts remaining.
