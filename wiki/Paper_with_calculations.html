Paper with calculations
General Information
Type :
Quest


The Paper with calculations is a single-use item which is required to fix the Mill. It can be obtained by talking to the Astrologer when continuing Ms. Charm's quest line. You need 60 Reputation with Astrologer to get the calculations.

The calculations have to be used at the door of the mill, not at the door of the mill-house.
