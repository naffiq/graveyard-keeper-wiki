The iron ore stockpile (formerly known as the "ore cutter") is a construct used to store iron blocks. It's required in order to later process these blocks at the stone cutter or stone cutter II into iron ore. It does not have any particular function of its own outside of this.

Notes

  • Only 7 blocks of iron can be stored in a stockpile at a time.
  • The one that can be built in the workyard is important, as the porter zombies use it to deposit iron blocks mined near the cabin in the woods.
