Iron block

Iron block
General Information
Type :
Primary Ore
Source(s)
Iron Ore Deposit


The iron block is a resource gathered from Iron Ore Deposit and needs to be stored in the Iron Ore Stockpile. It can be further processed with the Stone cutter or Stone cutter II to get Iron ore with a chance of acquiring silver or gold ore as well.
