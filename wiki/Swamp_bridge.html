Swamp bridge

Swamp bridge
Basic information
Location(s)
Swamp

The swamp bridge allows you to bypass the normally winding and circuitous path through the swamp and reach the witch's hut much faster.

It can take a substantial amount of to build the bridge and because of the long trip it's recommended to fill up your energy bar before leaving.
