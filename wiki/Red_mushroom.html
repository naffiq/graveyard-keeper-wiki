Must unlock Master Gatherer. It grows plentiful near swamps, such as the one north of your Sweet Home, and in the swamp near Clotho's hut. It can be sparsely found in some other places.

Can be found:

  • On the way to The Village second fence before Wheat Farm.
  • Before you cross the Swamp bridge north.
  • On the way to Clotho's hut next to the Swamp bridge.
  • North of your Sweet Home left side entrance Swamp Area.
  • Westside of the Village Pond.
