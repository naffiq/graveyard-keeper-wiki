The rusty sword equips the the slot and when used (Default spacebar) deals damage to enemies in front of you. It cannot be crafted and is given to you by the Blacksmith when he requests that you kill the slimes to the north. This happens after bringing him Horadric's note. The rusty sword begins in disrepair and must be fixed using the whetstone at the Grindstone. The rusty sword deals -5 damage.

Studying Rusty sword at the Study table will give you 10.
