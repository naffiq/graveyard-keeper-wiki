The Soul extractor III is a workbench introduced with the Better Save Soul DLC, that may be built in the Souls room after you unlock the Updated equipment technology. The Soul extractor III allows you to use a Corpse to extract its
Harmed soul for farther healing.
When the Harmed soul stays in a corpse for too long, it will eventually die and leave a Sin Shard behind. If this happens the corpse no longer can be used in a Soul extractor III.

To use the extractor, you place a body in it and click on the "Extractor soul" button in the crafting window. The craft is automatic and takes time. It can only be used once per corpse. With this process the extracted soul takes some damage based on the Soul extractor tier.
