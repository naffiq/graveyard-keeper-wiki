blood

Blood is a body part that can be extracted from a corpse at the autopsy table. Upon removal, it has the effect of removing one Red Skull and adding one White Skull , similar to fat. In addition, similar to most other anatomy and theology items, it can be studied at the Study table for a reward. For more information, see corpses.

An alternative for getting Blood aside from corpses is failing Alchemy recipes on purpose to obtain Blood Goo, which can be converted into Blood on the Hand Mixer. One example that will guarantee a blood goo is attempting to mix Life Powder, Oil and Water in the Alchemy Workbench II.
