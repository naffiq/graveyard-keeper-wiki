Beach

The beach is a quiet place east of the village and north of the lighthouse. It's a place where you spend time on your own.

It's not much of interest there. On the north end, there's a place to gather
sand.

In the center, there is a fishing spot, where you can walk out on a pier to fish in the sea.

In the south, not really belonging to the beach, there's a broken sea bridge. It used to be the bridge of the dead that led the dead towards the land of the dead before it was destroyed. It was made by the ancient god It's located directly at the end of the road that leads from the village eastwards.
