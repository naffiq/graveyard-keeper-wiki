The preparation place (or autopsy table) is where you prepare corpses or zombies by removing/adding body parts from/to them. Corpses placed upon the second tier preparation place will not decay at all unlike corpses left on the first tier preparation place. But more importantly, Tier II workstation reduces the chance of making a surgical error (combined with the respective perk the chance seems to become zero).


Each autopsy table increases the by one. See corpses, zombies and morgue for more information on the various morgue mechanics.

Surgeon's Mistake

Icon Label Description Notes
Surgeon's Mistake Your inexperience shows. - The surgery didn't go very well. You've made a mess of the body, lowering it's quality. Surgeon's mistakes occur randomly while removing a body part. It shows as a "body part" (see icon) that cannot be removed. The surgeon's mistake removes one additional and adds one additional . To avoid such mistakes in the future, get the butcher perk and the surgeon perk.
