Alchemy

Alchemy is linked with the production and use of powders, solutions, extracts, and other substances to produce more complex products.

Fishing

Fishing is related to the various factors related to catching fish.

Sermon

Sermons are the presentations the player can give in the church once it is unlocked.

Corpse

Corpses have an internal rating, many usable operations, and undergo constant decay. This page gives an overview of how these factors interact.

Farming

Farming is related to yields, rates, and qualities of crops and what modifies them. As well as where and how things must be grown.

Cooking

Cooking is related to the preparation of food using the oven and the cooking table.

Storage

Storage discusses how the various in game inventories and stockpiles interact and their capacities.

Mining

Mining is an overview of where to find various ores, stones, and other resources extracted from the earth.

Currency

Currency is a page dedicated to the discussion of the games money and how prices can vary from the established base price.
