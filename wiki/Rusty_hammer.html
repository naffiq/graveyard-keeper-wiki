The rusty hammer equips the the slot and is used when constructing blueprints and during several other select operations. The rusty hammer can be found in the graveyard trunk at the start of the game or can be purchased from the Blacksmith. The hammer tool progression was bugged as Rusty hammers could only be crafted at the anvil II and iron hammers require steel and have the steel hammer sprite in-game. It can now be crafted at the Anvil.

Efficiency represents how much progress toward an operation each swing of the Hammer provides. The rusty hammer provides the base 70% efficiency.

Energy usage represents how much energy each operation consumes. The rusty hammer requires -1 per swing.

Studying Rusty hammer at the Study table will give you 10.
