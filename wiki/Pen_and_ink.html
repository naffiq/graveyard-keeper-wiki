Pen and ink is a writing resource, that sometimes is treated as equipment with a durability (10 uses) and is sometimes consumed - ignoring its current durability - depending on a craft. Its primary use is in regards to writing. Note; a depreciated unit (ex; P&I with 1/10 dur) may still be used just as successfully in crafts that generally utilize a greater durability (ex; Flyer). Pen and Ink can be purchased from the Astrologer.
