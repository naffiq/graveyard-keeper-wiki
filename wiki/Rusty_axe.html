The rusty axe is equipped in the slot and is used when chopping down trees for logs or when splitting wood at the chopping spot. The rusty axe can be found in the graveyard trunk at the start of the game or can be purchased from the Blacksmith.

Efficiency represents how much progress toward an operation each use of a tool provides. An efficiency of 100% would be full speed, 200% would be double speed, and 50% would be half speed. The rusty axe is 70% efficient.

Energy usage reflects how much energy each action consumes. The rusty axe consumes 1 per swing.

Studying Rusty axe at the Study table will give you 10.
