Harmed soul

A Harmed soul is a new item presented in the Better Save Soul DLC. It is obtained through the Soul extractor from fresh corpse. Next step is cleansing a Harmed soul via Soul healer into a Healed soul, while also giving Sin shards depending on the number of bodyparts used.

Any Harmed soul left outside a Soul container will rapidly deteriorate into a Sin shard , including souls still in corpses. In turn, Healed souls will deteriorate much slower. It takes soul inside corpse to turn into sin shard about same time as corpse to decay by 20% if it on the floor of mortuary. Burying corpse in grave pauses soul degradation , so extracting soul from very old (start of the game) corpse possible if it was buried fast enough.

Soul decay in soul containers appear to be 12% per day in soul container I , 4-5% in soul container II and close to zero or none in soul container III (no observed deterioration after 2 days).
