12-Skull Corpse Preparation

A perfectly-prepared corpse with x12 can be created with the processes outlined below. Be aware that preparing a 12-skull corpse requires advanced alchemy, access to an embalming table, and late-game materials such as gold nuggets and death reagents. It's possible to create a corpse with x16 (x26 with Better Save Soul); however, As part of Game of Crone DLC, the maximum Gravesite decor has increased to x16 , and up to the maximum of x26 with the Better Save Soul DLC installed.

With no DLC, the best result possible is x13. However, grave decorations only go up to x12 so the additional has no benefit for a buried corpse. The best bodies can have upon arrival is x2 x6.

Baseline

This is the quintessential starting point. If a corpse does not yet have these characteristics, then alter the corpse to reach this point, if needed.

Starting with a 4R/5W corpse

  1. Perform a
    Lye injection     ++
  2. Perform a
    Glue injection     +
  3. Perform a
    Silver injection     -+
  4. Perform a
    Gold injection     -+
  5. Remove the
    Fat     -+
  6. Remove the
    Blood     -+

Starting with a 4R/6W corpse

  1. Perform a
    Lye injection
  2. Perform a
    Glue injection     (Optional; x13)
  3. Perform a
    Silver injection
  4. Perform a
    Gold injection
  5. Remove the
    Fat
  6. Remove the
    Blood
