Obsidian is a miscellaneous item, introduced by the DLC Stranger Sins, which can only be found when searching for a specific tavern artifact - Ancient Shackles. Its only known purpose is to be studied, which upon doing so will provide the item Research Obsidian, along with a significant tech point bonus.

The Research Obsidian item can be used instead of a silver star book for the Astrologist last event. Doing so, however, locks the possibility of earning the "Best seller" achievement for giving the Astrologer a gold star Book.
