Location(s)
Forest near the river, Cabin in the woods

The wooden lantern can only be built on a place for lantern, located in the forest near the river or near the cabin in the woods. You need to set up this place first.

Wooden lanterns automatically ignite at dusk and go off at dawn. They shed some light at the surroundings. They can be upgraded to stone lanterns after erecting them. Just stand near them and hit "E".

Note: Those belonging to the lantern network located in the forest can not be removed.
