Captain
Basic Information
Location :
 The Dead Horse
Role :
Captain

The Captain is an NPC. Interacting with the Captain while he's in The Dead Horse will simply cause him to say "Arrr. Bad luck. Not today!".

During the Teodoro's quest, should Keeper fail to protect the refugees, the captain's ship can be used to sail them away from King's wraith.
