A crate factory is a place where you can pack crates of various items to be sold on the market. You can build it only on a single spot between the workyard and the kitchen garden (which means it has access to storages from both!), using the workbench in the workyard.

The factory works similar to the packing table but has additional options for some kind of goods. It comes complete with a built-in elevator, pallets and a porter station in the cellar.

You can put two zombies at work to pack and transport your goods. This is how it looks when a zombie works outside at the station packaging your goods:

As soon as a crate is ready it's put on the elevator to the cellar where it's moved instantly from the output chute onto a crate pallet. From there, it can be picked up manually or by a second zombie worker to be transported to the trade office.
