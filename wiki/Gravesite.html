The gravesite is where you'll bury corpses. All you must do to designate an area as a grave place is select it as an option at the graveyard blueprint desk. Digging out a grave will use durability on your shovel. Each grave can take two decorations, one grave fence and one grave marker and each decoration will effect the of the graveyard as a whole.

The number of positive modifiers to a grave can benefit from is based on the number of and on the corpse buried there. A grave's positive score cannot exceed the number of , any modifiers added beyond that point will have no effect. Modifiers can be gained through decorations but also from the corpses themselves, attempting to remove a when none are available will cause a corpse to gain an intrinsic +1. For more information on corpse rating see corpse and for more information on preparing corpses see the morgue location page.
