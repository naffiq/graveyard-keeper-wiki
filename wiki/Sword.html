The sword is equiped in the slot and deals damage to enemies in front of the player when used. It deals -10 damage to enemies, making it a significant upgrade over the rusty sword, and can either be crafted at a metal anvil or purchased from Koukol near the mountain fort. Alternatively, as the required technology is found on a relatively insignificant 'branch', one may easily bypass it in favor of it's successor - the steel sword.
