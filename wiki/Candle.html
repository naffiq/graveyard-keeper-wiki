Candles are church-related consumables, able to be used in any of the available candelabras, in order to provide a temporary increase in Church Quality. There are multiple types of candles, each providing a bonus; the more expensive or complex the candle, the greater the bonus. As higher-tier candelabras each have more candle slots, one would need enough candles of a single type in order to activate the given candelabra.
