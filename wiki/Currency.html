Currency

Coin

Copper, Silver and Gold coins are the currency used.

1 Gold Coin = 100 Silver Coins
1 Silver Coin = 100 Copper Coins

So 1 Gold Coin = 10,000 Copper Coins

Price

For additional information on and how it impacts prices, visit reputation.

Quality

Copper, Silver, and Gold also represent item quality with Copper , Silver , and Gold stars.
