The Miller can be found in the northern section of the Wheat Farm during the day. He sells grain products. He is the brother of the Farmer. As his mill does not work, he is always found saying "I get so tired without any work to do." until the quest is completed.

Quests

Fixing the Mill

Doing Ms. Charm's quest line will eventually lead you to being asked to fix the broken mill. You have to talk to the miller first. Then seek help at the astrologer to figure out the
paper with calculations. Then return to the mill to complete the project.

Trades

Miller is bugged in that he is unable to resell
Firewood. Once he runs out of money, he'll be unable to restock his inventory. To remedy that, trade with him until his stock reaches the base amount (i.e. If he has 5×
Hops in stock, he'll start making money again).
