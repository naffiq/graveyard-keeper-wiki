The Undertaker is part of the Game of Crone DLC. She arrives at the Refugee camp after you built and filled the third tent. Her questline will be available the next day.

Quests

First, she asks you to make a few new tools ( 5×
Iron chisel, 10×
Wood wedge) for her because she left them in the Town. Finishing this quest unlocks trading tier I.

Next, she'll ask you to build a workplace for her at the camp. Finishing this quest unlocks trading tier II.

To enable her to make better quality gravestones, she asks you to bring 5×
steel chisel. Finishing this quest unlocks trading tier III and she'll give you
Recipe advanced gravestones,
Recipe advanced gravestones II.
