Beets are a form of crop which can be grown from beet seeds in the garden below the workyard known as the kitchen garden. Alternatively they can be purchased from the Farmer. Consuming beet provides 8. 12×
Beet are required for the Merchant's quest. They do not have to be grown by the player. Beets are not an improvable crop. It does not have quality levels and cannot be improved with fertilizer. It can only be made to grow faster. See farming for more information on how crops are grown and improved.
