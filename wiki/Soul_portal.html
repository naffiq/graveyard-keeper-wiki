The Soul portal is a workbench introduced with the Better Save Soul DLC, that may be built in the Souls room after the Soul healer is repaired. The Soul portal allows you to convert Healed souls to Souls' gratitude () points.
The number of points generated from this is based on the soul condition. These points () can be used to remote craft or to unlock new technologies.
