The preparation place (or autopsy table) is where you prepare corpses or zombies by removing/adding body parts from/to them. Corpses stored in the tier 1 preparation place will have their decay slowed but not stopped, while their decay will be stopped when stored in the tier 2 preparation place.

The morgue starts with one tier 1 table, but additional constructs may be built after the mortuary desk is repaired.

Each autopsy table increases the by one. See corpses, zombies and morgue for more information about the various mechanics related to the preparation of corpses.

Surgeon's Mistake

Icon Label Description Notes
Surgeon's Mistake Your inexperience shows. - The surgery didn't go very well. You've made a mess of the body, lowering it's quality. Surgeon's mistakes occur randomly while removing a body part. It shows as a "body part" (see icon) that cannot be removed. The surgeon's mistake removes one and adds one . To reduce the odds of this occurring, the player may take various precautions, such as unlocking the butcher perk and the surgeon perk and upgrading Preparation place to II

Insert Bodyparts

Icon Label Description Notes
Insert Bodyparts You can (re-)insert body parts to change the skull count of a body. Upon re-insertion, a body part will simply undo the "Effect of removal" for that part.
