Master Alaric

Alaric
Basic Information
Location :
 Refugee camp
Role :
Vampire

Alaric is an inhabitant of the Refugee camp and essential for the Vampire quest line. You can find him inside a tent. He will tell you emphatically he does not believe the tales of vampires, so you have to watch him.

Quests

Supply him with Blood

Alaric is weak because of starvation. He asks you for a drop of
Blood or
Bucket of blood. After delivering he will reward you with
Sun cream for vampires as a reward. This can be shown to Horadric to make him believe you took care of the vampire and to end the vampire quest line.

He also points out to use the
Witchers eye to find the vampires that turned him into one.

After his quest, supplying him with
Bucket of blood will get you a
Scroll of Teleportation. Or 25 for every
Blood.
