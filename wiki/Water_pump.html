Location(s)
Workyard, Refugee camp

Water pump is located in the Workyard, after building it is placed to a fixed spot next to the Well. Water Pump generates water slowly over time, which can be taken without needing any energy. Every 10 seconds, one drop of Water is generated into pump's inventory. Water can be taken out in 10-piece stacks until the inventory is depleted. The internal inventory capacity is 500 and can be accessed by any workbench in the workyard.
