Notes

  1. ↑ Shops may freeze their wares at random, meaning the tradeable supplies of an NPC may not reset according to natural rate. This can leave the player unable to progress due to the essential nature of some vendor specific items. The currently recommended fix is to load an earlier version of the game (Alpha 0.675 confirmed to work), allow an in-game day to pass, save, and then load back into Alpha 0.678. Loading an old version may have unintended consequences though including potentially breaking your save permanently so a backup is recommended before trying this.
  2. ↑ The Super Puper was a placeholder item from internal development that would drop in Alpha 0.0677 upon harvesting grapes.
  3. ↑ A black hole bug refers to a situation where the ground texture would not load leaving behind a black void.
