Universal bag was introduced in Game of Crone DLC. It can hold 9 items in it, effectively increasing your inventory size. There is also bigger version of it: Big universal bag. You cannot put one bag inside another bag. One bag and recipe for it is rewarded upon completing about 75% of the refugee camp quest line with Marquis Teodoro Jr.
