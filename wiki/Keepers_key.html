Keepers key is a quest item, obtained from the astrologer when he gives you a quest to retrieve the keepers journal. Upon using it at the study table with the instructions for key from Snake, the active key will be created, which can then be given to Snake to unlock the dungeon. Beyond this, there is no known purpose for this item.
