The axe II is equipped in the slot and is used when chopping down trees for logs or when splitting wood at the chopping spot. The axe II must be forged at the anvil II by the player and isn't available for sale.

Efficiency reflects how much progress toward a task each use of a tool provides. An efficiency of 100% would be full speed, 200% would be double speed, and 50% would be half speed. The axe II is 150% efficient.

Energy usage reflects how much energy each action consumes. The axe II consumes 1 per swing.

Studying Axe II at the Study table will give you 100.
