The chest is a storage device, very similar to the trunk, except its contents are limited to 20 (5x4) unique items - identical to the player's inventory, and one less row than the aforementioned trunk. Two of them are already built in the Sweet Home and those are indestructible. In addition to the Sweet Home chests, two of them can be built in the Church, and 2 more in the Souls room with the Better Save Soul DLC.
