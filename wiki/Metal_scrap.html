Metal scrap is a byproduct of breaking down any workstation that originally required metallic materials. Alternatively, it's also found within the Dungeon as dropped loot, either from defeating an Iron Maiden or by destroying certain objects. While it cannot be bought, sold, or studied, it can be smelted back into an Iron ingot using any available furnace.
