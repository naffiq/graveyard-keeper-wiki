The good fishing rod is a piece of equipment which, unlike most other tools, does not degrade with use. It's one of three fishing rods found in-game, any of which is required for fishing. It is provided by the Lighthouse Keeper, sold as a Tier II item for 3 . It cannot be acquired through any other means, such as crafting, nor can it be studied.

Compared to the other fishing rods, this fishing rod is the "all-purpose rod", with decent efficiency, and while it can catch all low-tier and most high-tier fish, one may find that it's not always ideal. However, for the most part, it's much more versatile than the 'Simple', while still having a few advantages over the 'Excellent'.
