Wooden sticks are a crafting material, at their most basic being the least efficient source for fuel; 10×
Wooden stick -> 20×
Fuel. Alternatively, they can be used in various building and crafting applications, such as being required to craft most tools. They are one of the few materials that cannot be crafted themselves; they must either be gathered from bushes (bushes give 2x), collected when cutting down trees (normal trees gives 1-2x, small trees gives 4x), or purchased from the wood vendor Tress.
