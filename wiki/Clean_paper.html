Clean paper can be obtained in multiple ways and is the basis of all written works. It can be crafted from pigskin paper at the church workbench, which is itself easily made with skin, or it can be made from paper glop in a paper press. Additionally it can be purchased from the Astrologer on . Paper is one of the items requested by Vagner, the actor and is necessary to write flyers for the Inquisitor. Paper is also very useful for converting into science for the purposes of performing research at the study table.
