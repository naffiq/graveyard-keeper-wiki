Old rusty key is a quest item from the Better Save Soul DLC. After unlocking the DLC, this item can be obtained during a new Gerry questline. This questline requires 1×
A mug of beer and 1×
River sand to unlock the Old rusty key item. (Note: No other quality beer is acceptable.)

This item can be used to continue the Euric questline to unlock the Souls room next to the Morgue.
