Ode for Bishop is a quest item from the Better Save Soul DLC. This item is needed to unlock the pride sin sard.

First, research the Healer enthusiast technology. This tech will unlock a storyline with Smiler, who will redirect you to Euric to find a way to obtain a special sins shard to heal Euric's pride. To do so, you need to get the Ode for Bishop item from Vagner and read its words to the Bishop. This part of the questline will consume the item.
