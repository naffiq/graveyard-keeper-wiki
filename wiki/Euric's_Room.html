Euric's Room

Exits
 Souls room
Npcs
Euric

Euric will move into a cellar under the Souls room, but you will need to repair the broken furniture in his room. Upon completeing the room's repairs, you will receive 1 and a large cathedral window that will be automatically installed in the Church. After using the charged sins on Euric, you can then add them to the cathedral window. Each of the charged sins will depict a scene from the history of the person you got each sin from.
