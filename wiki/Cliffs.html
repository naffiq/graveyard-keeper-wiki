Cliffs

The cliffs are a lonesome place, where the graveyard keeper can rest after a long day's toil and gaze out over the vast sea. This area is located in the far north east corner of the world, near the mountain fort and the ocean beach.

The cliffs wouldn't be worth mentioning at all, if it was not where you need to meet Vagner for a quest. Walking towards the area at the right time of day will trigger the cutscene.
