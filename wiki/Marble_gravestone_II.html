The marble gravestone II is a grave decoration which will provide a bonus to when placed on a grave place after a corpse has been buried there. The number of positive modifiers to a grave can benefit from is based on the number of and . A grave's positive score cannot exceed the number of on the corpse buried there, any decorations added beyond that point will have no effect. For more information on the types of graves and their effects see the Graveyard's location page.

Installing or uninstalling a grave decoration will take 5-10.
