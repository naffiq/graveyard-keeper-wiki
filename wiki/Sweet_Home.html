Sweet Home
Location information
Buildable
 False
Exits
 Cellar, Workyard

One of the two places where the player is able to sleep (the other being the Cabin in the woods). Contains a bed, two chests, the oven and the cooking table.

The hatch leads to the cellar and onward to several different areas, and is acessible after finishing the tutorial.

After the first night Yorick, the ghost, haunts you.

Using the worktable, you can change the existing decorations of your home. To create the paints used for the decorations, simply combine the powder of the corresponding colour with oil. There are eight new paints for the eight powders (not including white and black):

Paint Colours
Blue Brown Dark Green Green Red Dark Violet Violet Yellow
Acceleration Slowing Health Order Toxic Death Chaos Life


Save before decorating. The game seems to sometimes apply decorations upon selection at the worktable, before the left click confirmation at the preview page, so you may waste materials if you don't save.

Wallpapers

This changes the wallpaper of the room. All colors can be combined with all patterns. If you choose a wallpaper, you can see the result in a preview and only if you accept it, the wallpaper will be permanently changed. If you want to remove a pattern without changing it to another one, you can remove it via the "Remove" option at the workstation.
