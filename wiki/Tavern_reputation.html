Tavern reputation is a resource that's necessary in order to start events at the Talking Skull, never actually possessed by the player as an item, similar to how is consumed to perform certain crafting tasks at the oven or furnaces. To obtain Tavern reputation (), simply supply the Talking Skull tavern with Beer, Booze, Mead and Wine it will be automatically sold to the villagers. The higher the quality of the drinks, the more will be earned.
