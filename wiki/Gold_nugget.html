Gold nuggets are a fairly rare resource that can be obtained through a variety of methods. With the Jeweler technology, they can be processed into Gold ingots at a Furnace II or Furnace III. Once a single nugget has been studied, nuggets can be broken down into a Gold powder for use in alchemy.

Sources

  • While exploring the dungeon, you may come across:
    • gold ore veins, requiring a pickaxe to mine, each yielding 8 nuggets, or 9 if the Jeweler perk is unlocked. There are 4 veins in total, found on levels 6, 9, 14 and 15.
    • Golden Golems on level 15. There are 4 golems, dropping 2-5 nuggets each.
  • By mining and processing iron ore after opening the Precious Metals technology:
    • mining iron ore blocks from the Iron Deposit, located in the north-west corner of the map, near the Quarry. Low chance, approximately 10% per block with the Miner perk (tested on PC version 1.407).
    • processing these blocks into ore at a stone cutter. Very low chance, approximately 1% per block, for both Stone cutter and Stone cutter II (tested on PC version 1.407).
  • By processing the gold crucian fish at the cooking table. While catching them can be difficult, each fish yields 1 nugget.
  • From the Moneylender in the Refugee camp for 22 (with the Game of Crone DLC).

It is not possible to find any precious metals in the bog iron deposits.

If you have difficulties finding gold nuggets, consider buying their derivatives instead:

  • the Merchant sells Gold jewelry details;
  • the Moneylender, available with the Game of Crone DLC, sells Gold jewelry details, Gold ingots and Gold powder.

For example, lots of Gold jewelry details are required to make a full set of top-tier candelabras: Candelabra III and Wall candelabra III. It might be easier to buy the details than to gather the nuggets needed to craft them.
