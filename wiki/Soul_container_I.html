The Soul container I is a workbench introduced with the Better Save Soul DLC, that may be built in the Souls room after you unlock the Beginner healer technology. The Soul container I allows you to store up to 2 souls for later usage, but keep in mind the souls will decay slowly even in this container. Decay rate is around 12% per day . Higher tier containers appear to have slower decay rate , ~ 4% for tier II container (as of version 1.404 , just on one observation ; not precise measurement).

To use one of these containers first, you have to build a frame first [Soul container I (Base)], then fill it up by finishing the build with extra materials [Soul container I (Finish)]. There are only 3 spots in the Souls room where this container can be built.

Note: When a soul stays in the container for too long, it will eventually die and leave a Sin Shard behind.
