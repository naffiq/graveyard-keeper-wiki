Flask

Flask
General Information
Type :
Stranger Sins Quest item
Source(s)
Farmer's Son


The Flask (or Ancient wine flask) is retrieved from the Farmer's Son. He's surprised that you know he drinks wine from the Flask since he drinks in secret. He'll ask you not to tell his father about it.

Optional: If you don't tell the Farmer that his son is drinking, he will have a cut scene discussion with you as you exit your Sweet Home after sleeping. He'll thank you for not telling him. He's happy that his son is growing up and has the gumption to defy him.

If you tell him however, he tells you the flask is his and requests 5 in payment.

The flask can be given to Gerry at the Talking skull for the second cutscene, "Terror is coming" in the Archaelogical time machine.
