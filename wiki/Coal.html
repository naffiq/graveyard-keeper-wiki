The coal must be extracted with a pick. The only known deposits are on the north cliff face near the quarry, in the forest northwest of the graveyard and south of the wheat farm. It can also be obtained via a zombie ore mine.

The southern deposit is unreachable, due to it being in the town, yet the icon is still given to mine when approached from above.

To access the northern area, you must rebuild the stone bridge west of the graveyard, and the debris that blocks the road will have to be cleared.

A second possibility is to clear the blockage north of the apiary and use the footbridge to cross the river.

Each time you mine the deposit you will get between 20 and 30 coal. It can be used as a fuel source in an oven or melted into graphite in a furnace.
