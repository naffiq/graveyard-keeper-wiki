Flyers are a relatively generic quest item, used in a variety of applications, usually quest-related. The Inquisitor asks the player to provide 10x for a quest. They are also used for marketing campaigns and tavern events. Creating them requires the Business of Faith Technology from the Theology tree.

Tips & Tricks

Regarding the mass-production of flyers, despite crafting them at a desk requiring feathers, and significantly more energy and time, the printing press is incredibly inefficient when it comes to ink. For every 10x flyers crafted, utilizing pen-and-inks will consume 1/3 of the ink that a printing press will, if not less (one may use <50% pen-and-inks). Thus, if one has a severe shortage of black paint, avoid using the printing press, despite it being more efficient overall.
