Moneylender is an NPC vendor, introduced with the Game of Crone DLC. He will appear at the refugee camp after the player summons a third 'new refugee', for a total population of 7. In addition to his trades, he offers a one-time 1 loan at 1% daily interest, which you can pay back at any point upon returning to the camp during the daytime and talking with him. You will be unable to trade with him while you have outstanding debt.
