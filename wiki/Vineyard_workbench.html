Vineyard workbench
Basic information
Location(s)
Vineyard

The vineyard workbench appears after you advance the inquisitor's quest a bit. It enables you to grow more vine there and store your stuff in a trunk.

Otherwise, you can only clear out the old trellises and use them for growing grapes and hops.

It is located southwest of Witch Hill and east of Sweet home.
