Cooking Table

The cooking table cannot be built and is unlocked from the beginning of the game.

Unlike the oven, the cooking table mostly produces food components, which can be used for more complicated meals. A few items are cold dishes and can be eaten directly. Even some non-food items like urns can be crafted here.

The cooking table has 2 skins, normal and updated.

Normal skin: 4×
flitch, 6×
Nails, 3×
Simple iron parts

Updated skin 4×
wooden plank, 6×
Nails, 3×
complex iron parts

Oven

The oven is found in your home and is where you will do most of the cooking in the game. The oven is one of few work stations which cannot be constructed.

Note: To use the oven for cooking, you have to fuel it in advance. The oven prepares food in a few seconds (the numbers are real world time in seconds). You can use batch production to order several meals in one turn.

Some food can be crafted at 3 quality levels. Copper, silver, and gold stars. They yield different amounts when consumed.

Note: To use the Professional Oven for cooking, you have to fuel it in advance. The oven prepares food in a few seconds (the numbers are real world time in seconds). You can use batch production to order several meals at a time.

Some food can be crafted at 3 quality levels. Copper, silver, and gold stars. They yield different effects and amounts when consumed.
