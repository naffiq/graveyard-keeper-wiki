The random text generator is a zombie workstation, able to be built within the alchemy lab after the player unlocks the appropriate technology. This particular workstation specializes in written works.

Like all zombie workstations, the player cannot operate it themselves, but a zombie can be stationed at the workstation at the player's discretion, in order to craft anything selected by the player in a queue, the products of which are deposited into a nearby container.
