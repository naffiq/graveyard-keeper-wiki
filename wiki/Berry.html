Berries are a consumable product. They can only be harvested from berry bushes, found throughout the wilderness or planted by the player in the orchard. If the Game of Crones dlc is installed, they may also be occasionally found in the storehouse of the Refugee Camp. They can be used directly to restore +5 or alternatively in cooking-related recipes.
