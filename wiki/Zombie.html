Zombie

Making a zombie can be unlocked relatively early in the game by completing a series of short missions and unlocking the right technologies. You first have to meet Gunter and take his advice.

Gunter and Zombies were added in the Breaking Dead DLC and become available past midnight of the fourth  (19.5 days)

The downsides are that you will not get any Technology points from the working zombies. To counter that you can:

  • Let zombies write point books at the Random text generator
  • Buy books from the Astrologer
  • Research graveyard items or advanced tools
  • Use Circumspect to increase points for research

Creating a zombie

Zombies are made on a resurrection table with a corpse, 1×
zombie juice, and 10×
Faith. To put a zombie to work, carry it to a work station and place it where prompted. have no effect on zombies, and can be added to zombies later.


Zombie juice is crafted at an alchemy workbench (tier I). Constructing this requires two advanced conical flasks, which in turn require unlocking Glass-blower II and Advanced Smelting for 150 50 50. These prerequisites pose a significant barrier in the early game. In the meantime, 3×
zombie juice can be found in Gunter’s cell.

Dispose a zombie

It is possible to throw zombies into the river or bury them in the graveyard to dispose of subpar or not needed zombies. It is advisable to remove all before doing so. Zombies get no penalty from but a grave does.

Improving a zombie

Zombies have a stat called "Work Efficiency" that represents how effective their work generally is. This stat directly relates to the number of skulls, while skulls will have no effect. A zombie can be modified, much like a regular corpse, as many times as is necessary either by autopsy at the preparation place or injections at the embalming table.

Efficiency is compared to the players crafting speed. For example a task that take 2s if done by the player will take 20s for a 10% efficiency zombie.

Efficiency doesn't have any impact on transport speed. Thus, it is a good idea not to waste injections or energy on preparing zombies that are going to be used in a Porter station.

Preparing a 16 Skull Corpse

  • Step 1: insert a brain, intestine, and heart with the attributes to get a corpse with 4 and 9 to start with. This can be any combination, for instance: (Brain -2/+3; Intestine +3/+3; Heart +3/+3)
  • Step 1.5 (optional): Use
    Dark injection     to add red skulls if necessary to reach 4 and 9 .
  • Step 2:
    Lye injection     to get 5 red skulls and 10 white skulls.
  • Step 3:
    Glue injection     to get 5 red skulls and 11 white skulls.
  • Step 4:
    Silver injection     to get 4 red skulls and 12 white skulls.
  • Step 5:
    Gold injection     to get 2 red skulls and 14 white skulls.
  • Step 6: Remove Blood to get 1 red skulls and 15 white skulls.
  • Step 7: Remove Fat to get 0 red skulls and 16 white skulls.

Preparing a 26 Skull Corpse (DLC only)

With the Better Save Soul DLC, the player is able to improve the rating of regular organs (Blood, Fat, Skin, Etc.) up to a maximum of +3 / through the use of Sin Shards. Follow the guide to get the maximum of 26. You can begin with more than listed, having leftover at the end, which does not negatively impact zombies.

  • Step 1: Remove all organs except skull, replacing the blood, fat, skin, and flesh with 3 versions of each.
  • Step 2: Insert a brain, intestine, and heart with the combined attributes of 2 and 9 . This can be any combination, for instance: (Brain 0/+3; Intestine +2/+3; Heart +0/+3). This will get you to a corpse with 2 and 21 .
  • Step 3:
    Lye injection     to get 3 and 22 .
  • Step 4:
    Glue injection     to get 3 and 23 .
  • Step 5:
    Silver injection     to get 2 and 24 .
  • Step 6:
    Gold injection     to get 0 and 26 .
