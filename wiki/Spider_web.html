Spider web is obtained by killing spiders in the dungeon. Its only known purpose is as a product of alchemy; once studied, it can be broken down into the Slowing variants of powder and extract.

Alchemy

Spider web can be decomposed to powder and extract with the alchemy mill and either distillation cube, respectively. Having unlocked Master of Alchemy produces one additional reagent at any workstation.
