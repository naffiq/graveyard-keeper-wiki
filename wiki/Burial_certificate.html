This burial certificate is an item that will be regularly awarded to the player each time they bury or cremate a corpse, while pointedly not being awarded when the corpse is disposed of in the nearby river. Its only known purpose is to be sold to Horadric, the innkeeper of The Dead Horse, at a consistent price of 1 50 each.
