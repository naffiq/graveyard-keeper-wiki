The stone stockpile is a construct used to store stone blocks and marble blocks. It's required in order to later process these blocks at the stone cutter or stone cutter II into stone pieces and marble pieces, respectively.

It's important, as zombies can deliver blocks there. Either they mine them, or they transport them from one stockpile to another.
