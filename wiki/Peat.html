Peat is produced by composting crop waste. It can be used to fertilize crop spaces such as empty garden beds, garden beds with sticks, and vine trellises. Peat acts as a boost and quality fertilizer. Crops grown with Peat will have their growth time reduced by 20% and will yield +1 seed and +1 product when harvested. For more information about the effects and use of fertilizer see farming.

Peat is available for purchase from the Shepherd.
