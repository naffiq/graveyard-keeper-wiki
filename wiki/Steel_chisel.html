A steel chisel is a degradable piece of equipment, used to refine various resource materials.

Each use costs 10% durability, before it is eventually destroyed, and further refinement requires that another be crafted. Exception is the Steel chisel upgrade craft which consumes the chisel ingredient and replaces it with a chisel at full durability.

It is one of the few craftable items to have various qualities, which in turn has an effect on the resource materials it refines. Alternatively, a quality chisel may be sold to Tress, without durability factoring in.
