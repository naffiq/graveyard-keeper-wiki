Refugee camp

Refugee camp
Location information
Buildable
 True
Exits
 Forest near the river
Npcs
Marquis Teodoro Jr., Cook, Moneylender, Undertaker, Tanner

The Refugee camp is activated once you meet Marquis Teodoro Jr. He appears at your bedside when you wake up, the first time you sleep after unlocking the Church. He takes you to the camp, which is located in Forest near the river (directly North of Sweet Home).

Here you are able to increase the camp population, meet new NPCs with new questlines to earn rewards such as the Universal bag and so on. To build any new structures and progress through the quests, first, you need to make the refugees happy.


Refugee Happiness is calculated by combining the happiness and the
refugee camp capacity. The camp starts with a base 0 out of 3×
Refugee Happiness.
Refugee Happiness decreases if food and water are missing and is capped by the
refugee camp capacity, which can be increased by camp development.

Food and Water Consumption

Each person in the camp consumes a total of 3×
water and 30 of food each day. There are 5 time intervals per day (one every 90 seconds). In each time interval, 6 of food and 0.6 water per person are removed from the stockpile.

The game consumes the items with the most value until the amount of energy required is reached. For example, when the camp has 4 refugees, it needs to consume 24, if there's cake in the stockpile, it will consume 1 cake, wasting 76. The same applies to water.

Quests

Cook appears once you build the Fence Supplies For Garden area and gives task of building her a kitchen (crafts food automated from the storage).

Undertaker appears after you built and filled the third tent. She trades gravestones and recipes to build the more advanced gravestones.

After building and filling the fourth tent Tanner will appear and ask you to build a workplace for her. She sells different kind of bags.

Camp upgrades

Each upgrade requires sufficient refugee satisfaction as well as the standard materials.
