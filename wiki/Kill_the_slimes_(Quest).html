Kill the slimes (Quest)

Note: At the moment, individual quest pages won't work due to quests not having proper names. For now quests should be described in the respective NPC page.

After delivering Horadric's letter to the blacksmith Krezvold he will ask you to kill some Slimes. To do this he gives you a broken rusty sword, two Energy potions, and a whetstone. You are meant to use the whetstone to repair the sword and then kill the Slimes with it. Afterward you need only bring the green jelly back to the Blacksmith. Only after you complete this Quest will you be able to trade with the blacksmith.

Requirements

Letter to the blacksmith 		Obtained from Horadric upon first meeting him.
2×
Green jelly 		Obtained by killing the two slime's north of the blacksmith.
