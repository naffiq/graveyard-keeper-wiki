Fat is a body part that can be extracted from a corpse at the autopsy table. Upon removal, it has the effect of removing one Red Skull and adding one White Skull , similar to blood. In addition, similar to most other anatomy and theology items, it can be studied at the Study table for a reward. For more information, see corpses.
