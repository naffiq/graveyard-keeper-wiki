Cabin in the woods

The cabin in the woods is a cozy new place that was introduced in the full release of the game.

The player may sleep inside (and save the game) as there is a bed available.

Unlocking

There are 2 ways to unlock this area:

  • After you repair the stone bridge that leads to the swamp, you follow the road until it ends. You'll find a path that's blocked by some rocks. You need to clear that path and go straight north.
  • Follow the path north of the graveyard (or north-west of your home), into the forest. There's a path that's blocked by some rocks. Once you clear that path, go north-west again, following the river, there will be a small footbridge that allows you to cross the river.

Mining

There are five infinite resources available for mining in this area (from west to east):

  • One big iron ore deposit, which gives Iron block when mined.
  • One big stone quarry, which gives Stone block when mined. You will need 4×
    Wood wedge     for two blocks. You can also employ zombies here.
  • One big marble quarry (has to be built in advance), to allow gathering of Marble block. You will need 4×
    Wood wedge     for two blocks. You can also employ zombies here.
  • One combined
    Iron ore     and
    Coal     deposit, where you can set up a zombie ore mine as soon as you've unlocked zombies.
  • One big coal deposit, which gives 20-30
    Coal     when mined.

Workstations

The workbench for placing all the workstations is located in the northwest corner of the workyard. For most stations, you have to unlock the related technology first.
