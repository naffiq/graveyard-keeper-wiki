The distillation cube II distills substances into extracts for use in alchemy. A substance cannot be processed in the distillation cube II until it has been researched at the study table. After, it lists decomposition products and anything that lists an essence as one of its decomposition products and can be processed in the distillation cube II. Using the Distillation Cube II yields more material than Tier 1 and is considerably faster.
